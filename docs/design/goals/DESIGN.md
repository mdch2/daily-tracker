## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, focused environment engineered for personal constancy, routine architecture, and habit mastery across iOS and Android platforms. 

### Brand Personality & Mood
- **Calm Mastery:** The interface recedes into an obsidian background, allowing user routines and data density to stand out without cognitive strain or visual clutter.
- **Energized Intent:** Precise injections of electric lime generate kinetic momentum. Rather than overwhelming the canvas, accent color is deployed strictly as an intentional focal point for completion, positive streaks, and immediate primary actions.
- **Systematic & Objective:** Avoids childish gamification, aggressive guilt tactics, or saturated gradients. The visual language evokes precision instruments, developer contribution heatmaps, and executive dashboards.

### Design Movement: Modern Technical Tactility
The visual philosophy pairs dark-mode minimalism with disciplined surface elevations and micro-tactile boundaries. It eschews both stark flat brutalism and heavy skeuomorphic effects. Instead, depth is achieved via layered charcoal tiers, crisp hairline containment borders (`1px`), and soft, ultra-focused interaction surfaces designed for thumb-reach ergonomical ergonomics.

## Colors

The color architecture is built to maintain contrast ratios compliant with WCAG AA/AAA across both dark and light modes.

### Dark Theme (Default)
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0F0E`):** Deep obsidian charcoal with a faint warm green undertone, eliminating pure `#000000` harshness.
- **Card & Container Surfaces (`#151A18`, `#1A211E`):** Multi-tiered surfaces bounded by micro-borders (`#242E29`) to preserve shape definition without relying on intrusive shadows.
- **Primary Kinetic Accent (`#C7DF65`):** High-energy electric lime reserved strictly for completed milestones, key CTAs, and active navigation nodes. All text placed inside filled primary lime buttons must use `#0D1110` to guarantee high legibility.

### Light Theme
- **Base Canvas (`#F7F8F5`):** Warm mineral off-white designed to reduce eye strain compared to harsh `#FFFFFF`.
- **Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Clean white cards with light structural borders (`#E4E8E3`).
- **Interactive Text & Anchors (`#3A6615` / `#4E7C1B`):** Deep botanical greens that provide sufficient contrast against light surfaces for non-button interactive text. Primary filled action buttons maintain `#C7DF65` with `#111714` typography.

### Habit Categorization vs. Semantic Feedback
Secondary colors are dedicated to habit categories and multi-hued heatmaps:
- Habit categories leverage **Emerald**, **Cyan**, **Amber**, **Violet**, and **Coral**.
- **System Error (`#EF4444`)** and **Success (`#22C55E`)** are strictly isolated to validation messages, toast notifications, and retry badges, always accompanied by unambiguous iconography to prevent confusion with habit category colors.

## Typography

The typography is built around **Manrope**, selected for its geometric clarity, open counters, robust numeric figures, and multi-language diacritics.

### Hierarchy & Scale Rules
- **Numerical Figures & KPI Metrics:** Rendered using `display-stat` with tabular numerals enabled (`tnum`) to ensure data alignment across habit streaks and check-in counters.
- **Headlines & Section Prompts:** Restricted to concise sentence case. Line heights accommodate descending glyphs from English and accented Spanish characters (á, é, í, ó, ú, ñ, ¿, ¡).
- **Secondary Legends & Captions:** Use medium weights (`500`) at 11–13px to maintain legibility on dark card surfaces without requiring high luminance contrast that could cause halation.
- **Bilingual Adaptability:** Form and action containers must allocate 25% minimum horizontal expansion tolerance to seamlessly accommodate character expansions between English and Spanish terms (e.g., "Log" vs. "Registrar", "Weekly Summary" vs. "Resumen semanal").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is tailored for single-hand mobile interactions within standard viewport bounds (360px to 430px base, with 390 × 844px as baseline reference).

### Grid & Density Rules
- **4-Point Grid Alignment:** All component dimensions, paddings, and structural margins adhere to increments of 4px (`0.25rem`).
- **Canvas Margins:** Screen boundaries enforce a lateral margin of `20px` (`1.25rem`) on mobile, expanding to `24px` (`1.5rem`) on larger device viewports.
- **Card Spacing:** Distinct cards and habit groupings use `12px` to `16px` vertical gaps (`space-sm` to `space-md`), creating unified visual clusters without losing track of individual rows.
- **Touch Reach Architecture:** Primary interactive anchors, quick-log buttons, and tab switches sit in the bottom two-thirds of the viewport, well within natural thumb reach.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on structural tonal boundaries and subtle luminescence rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Level (Elevation 0):** The foundational layer (`#0B0F0E` in dark mode, `#F7F8F5` in light mode).
- **Structural Card Level (Elevation 1):** Primary content containers (`#151A18` dark, `#FFFFFF` light) framed by a crisp `1px` outline using `border-dark-subtle` (`#242E29`) or `border-light-subtle` (`#E4E8E3`). Shadows are minimized: `0px 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)` in dark mode.
- **Interactive & Floating Level (Elevation 2):** Bottom navigation bars, modal sheets, and quick-action triggers use `#1A211E` with a hairline top border and a diffuse ambient shadow: `0px 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Lime Glow & Active States:** Selected cells and successful habit completions apply a localized ambient luminescence (`0 0 12px rgba(199, 223, 101, 0.25)`), providing direct visual feedback without shifting card boundaries.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined curvature scale that pairs soft exterior contours with compact interior controls.

### Curvature Hierarchy
- **Primary Data & Metric Cards:** Radii range from `20px` to `24px` (`rounded-xl`), creating inviting, modern content blocks.
- **Interactive Controls & Input Rows:** Habit items, text fields, and standard buttons employ `12px` to `16px` (`rounded-md` to `rounded-lg`) corner radii for balanced containment.
- **Filter Tags & Chips:** Full circular radii (`9999px` / pill-shaped) are strictly applied to week day pickers, category filters, and status badges.
- **Heatmap Matrix Cells:** Daily contribution cells utilize a miniature `3px` to `4px` corner radius, retaining the grid-like technical feel of developer commit charts while keeping corners soft.

## Components

### Habit Row (Core Daily Tracker Component)
- **Geometry:** Height 64px to 72px, corner radius 16px, background `#151A18`, 1px border `#242E29`.
- **States:**
  - *Pending:* Neutral category icon, habit title in `#FFFFFF`, state caption "Pendiente", check button with `#242E29` border and transparent interior. Touch target >= 48×48px.
  - *Saving (Guardando):* Subtle pulse animation on border; check button displays a 16px circular progress spinner in `#C7DF65`.
  - *Registered (Éxito):* Check button transitions to filled `#C7DF65` with dark `#0D1110` check icon; faint lime glow across the trailing boundary; title remains distinct.
  - *Error with Retry (Fallo):* Border transitions to `#EF4444`, subtitle switches to "Error al guardar. Reintentar", trailing action exposes an amber/red circular refresh icon.

### Heatmap Matrix (Activity Grid)
- **Cell Structure:** Matrix of 7 rows (representing days Mon–Sun) and up to 16–26 compact columns.
- **Cell Scale:** 10×10px to 12×12px with 3px gaps.
- **Visual Levels:**
  - Level 0 (Empty/Unrecorded): `#18201C` (Dark), `#E9EDE7` (Light).
  - Level 1–4 (Intensity/Duration): Graded transitions from muted emerald/lime tones up to solid `#C7DF65`.
  - Inactive/Not Scheduled: Outlined with no fill.
  - Future Days: Muted dashed border or 20% opacity.

### Segmented Time Range Pickers
- **Container:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height 40px, filled with `#151A18`, containing options: "Semana", "Mes", "Año" (or "Week", "Month", "Year").
- **Selected State:** Active segment transitions to solid `#C7DF65` with bold `#0D1110` label text. Inactive segments show `#8E9B94` text with smooth hover/touch states.

### Primary & Secondary Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Full-width or inline, height 52px, corner radius 14px, solid `#C7DF65` fill, text `action-label` set in `#0D1110` Bold. Minimum touch target 52×48px.
- **Secondary/Ghost:** Height 52px, transparent background, 1px border `#242E29`, text `#FFFFFF` or `#C7DF65`.

### Numeric Metric Cards (Progress & Stats)
- **Layout:** Grid of 2 columns, height auto (min 110px), padding 16px.
- **Content:** Header label (`caption-medium`) with muted icon at top-right, bold primary KPI value (`display-stat-mobile`), and a bottom micro-indicator (e.g., ring progress, linear bar, or delta percentage `+9%`).

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- **Dimensions:** Fixed height 64px + device safe area inset, background `#0B0F0E` with 85% opacity and backdrop-filter blur (20px). Top border 1px `#242E29`.
- **4 Tab Destinations:** "Hoy", "Hábitos", "Progreso", "Más" (or "Today", "Habits", "Progress", "More").
- **Active Indicator:** Icon and caption switch to `#C7DF65`; inactive tabs rest at `#667870`. Touch target per tab measures 64×52px.

### Bottom Sheet & Detail Modals
- **Structure:** Rounded top corners (24px), background `#151A18`, top grab handle (36×4px, `#324039`, centered 8px from top).
- **Contents:** Habit configuration, day inspection readout, and language/appearance preference selectors with explicit radio checkmarks and non-dismissive cancel/save footers.
